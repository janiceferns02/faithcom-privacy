# Privacy Policy – Faithcom

**Effective Date:** 23 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

Faithcom is a Christian prayer application that provides prayers and daily readings to help users with their faith and spiritual journey.

This Privacy Policy explains how Faithcom handles information when you use the application.

## 2. Information We Collect

Faithcom does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Faithcom does not ask users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or personal prayer requests. The app does not provide a feature for submitting personal prayer requests or journal entries.

The app stores your selected language preference locally on your device so that it can display content in your preferred language. This preference is not associated with a user account.

## 3. App Content

Faithcom provides prayers and daily readings as part of its app content. This content may be retrieved from our content service and stored locally on your device to make it available within the app.

This content is provided by Faithcom and is not personal information submitted by users.

## 4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Faithcom uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect or process certain information from your device and your interaction with advertisements. Depending on your device settings, location, consent choices and applicable requirements, this may include:

- IP address
- Device and advertising identifiers
- Information about app and advertising interactions, such as ad views or taps
- Diagnostics and performance information

This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention and related services.

Faithcom also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices and consent where required.

Google processes information in accordance with its own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information handled by Faithcom and its advertising service providers may be used to:

- Remember your selected app language on your device.
- Provide prayers and daily readings.
- Display and measure advertisements.
- Support advertising consent and privacy choices.
- Help maintain app security and reliability and prevent advertising fraud.

## 6. Information Sharing

Faithcom does not sell users' personal information.

Faithcom does not collect personal profile information through account registration or user-submitted prayer features.

Google AdMob may process device and advertising-related information as described in Google's privacy policies. Its handling of that information is governed by Google's policies and applicable privacy requirements.

## 7. Data Storage, Security and Retention

Your language preference is stored locally on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling Faithcom.

Faithcom does not maintain user accounts or personal profiles.

Information processed by Google AdMob is handled and retained according to Google's applicable policies.

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Permissions

Faithcom does not require access to your camera or photo gallery for its functionality.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Faithcom is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed or marketed to children under the age of 13.

Faithcom does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. Advertising services may process device or advertising-related information as described in this policy and subject to applicable consent and privacy requirements.

If you believe that information has been collected from a child in a way that should not have occurred, please contact us.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app, its services or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
